WireFromToNote

Concept stage, laid out after the perfboard best-practice research of 2026-10-23: parts first, short straight wires, nothing under the socketed ESP32, buses in a clear lane next to their users, 24 V isolated with capacitors at the drivers. A few crossings are unavoidable on one side; each becomes a two-hole hop to the back side. Exact hole-by-hole positions, the hops and the solder order come in the soldering plan, after T10 and T11 pass on the breadboard. ESP32 pin positions follow the printed labels on the Freenove board; the 5V pin position (USB end) is to be checked on the board.
