Concept stage, laid out after the perfboard best-practice research of 2026-10-23: parts first, short straight wires, nothing under the socketed ESP32, buses in a clear lane next to their users, 24 V isolated with capacitors at the drivers. Every part leg and every wire end sits in its own hole; where a wire must meet a header pin it uses the neighbouring hole plus a solder bridge on the back (the board's holes are plated through, so a bridge or a wire on either side connects). Where a wire would cross another on the same side, it hops to the other side for a few holes. Exact hole numbers and the solder order come in the soldering plan, after T10 and T11 pass on the breadboard.
